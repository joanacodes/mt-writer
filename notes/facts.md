# Facts the host has shared — the only source of personal material

## Who
- Reads and teaches tarot from Paris. Bilingual. Started 2020, reading for around a hundred people at first, later ~300 blind readings for strangers, mostly online (Discord servers, group calls, a live), some face to face, all free. Kept a notebook: what she said / what was confirmed. About nine in ten held; about one in ten missed. Manual count, not a study.
- Sells lessons: one-to-one sessions (60 min video), workshops (3 h, 12 people, one lens each), free events with public readings drawn at random. Never sells readings.
- Sold sage, candles and "channelled" cards in 2021; stopped. Nothing in it improved a reading.
- Refuses: health, death, pregnancy, ongoing court cases, money decisions, curses, third parties.
- Teaches five lenses instead of 78 meanings: colours, four elements, numerology (the 1–10 cycle told through the story of Instagram), feminine/masculine energies, astrological modes (cardinal/fixed/mutable). Astrology and symbols as extra lenses.
- Deck: the 1909 Rider-Waite-Smith, drawn by Pamela Colman Smith. Card images public domain.

## Stories (use only these, only as told)
- First reading that "missed": on a Discord group found via Tumblr, she read for a stranger about a relationship; the woman said it didn't resonate, she wasn't looking for a relationship. Three or four days later the woman texted, shocked: everything had happened, and the reading had also shown she hadn't been kind to the other person.
- The grandfather: after reading for a friend, she felt someone with her at home — the friend's grandfather, a strict Japanese man with much love in him. She said "I said I don't want anyone in my house… sir." He seemed to speak Japanese, so she drew instead: the number five, repeatedly, and "under the house". When she asked "we're not done, are we?" she got an irritated "Sorewa!" ("that's it"). The friend went to the basement storage cell number five of her building (not hers, already open) and found it full of paint pots. The whole message: there's a lot of paint in cell five.
- Her father: while she lived in London (2019), she heard him call her name as if from the next room; he was in Paris at his Buddhist temple, praying and thinking of her.
- The yellow ribbon: harsh headaches that switched on and off when she approached her desk; she tried meditation for the first time, and within seconds saw a yellow ribbon and understood "you're diverging — go home". She moved back to France. Five months later, Covid.
- The vivid dreams of late 2020: every night, a gentle gigantic tsunami telling her to surrender (she didn't; she hid in a house with others); a dream of dying, and disappointment at waking.
- The voice: enraged after her closest friend died of cancer, she asked silently "who's next?" and heard a man's voice give another friend's name and "is going to have an accident". Two weeks later that friend's car was hit by a truck. The friend is alive and well today; the information was wrong in what mattered. Lesson: timing, interpretation, and a shelf labelled "sceptical, but if it happens, I was warned".
- Astrology: she put on an astrology video to mock it, read her own chart, it was too precise. She tested her chart box by box (tick or cross), then family, friends, strangers, and her admired friend (whose chart was on point and harsh; she said "your life was hard but it feels like you like it somehow"; he said it was right in a way). Overall 30–40%; Saturn, Lilith, Chiron 75–90%.
- Pick-a-card: a Kloee Taylor pick-a-card video made her sob; it put words on her situation for the first time; the reader cried too. She calls it the beginning of her "awakening" and also finds the word cringe and misused.
- Her friends bought her first deck (from her wishlist) the Christmas after; she'd expected an astrology book.
- Teaching origin: she made friends and family try reading; the moment they say something true and are shocked by what they said is why she teaches. It can't be told, only experienced.
- Reviews on Facebook: a reader got "chills when you mentioned spells"; another was read during a live.
- She sometimes stops after two hours of readings, out of caution.

## Numbers she uses
- 300 readings, ~90% held, ~10% missed (by her own count).
- Astrology: 30–40% overall; 75–90% Saturn, Lilith, Chiron.
- Workshops: 3 hours, 12 people. Sessions: 60 minutes.

## Positions
- No Chosen Ones. Anyone can learn.
- Tarot is not an authority. Act on what you feel.
- Cards aren't magic; you are.
- Take a break from your cards (dependency).
- Not everything is about spirits; sometimes it's psychological, sometimes more — a therapist's door.
- Astrology is not an excuse.
- A miss is a miss: no reframing, no clarifier card.
- The silence rule: the sitter tells her nothing first.
